## 1. PROJECT SUMMARY

Build a focused, conversion-oriented microsite for **one specific Stream Conferences event**. Its entire job is to get a visitor to: understand the conference at a glance → submit an abstract → register → find logistics (venue, program, itinerary). It's information-dense but must stay scannable, credible, and easy to navigate — this is the page academics, clinicians, and industry delegates use to decide whether to submit work and attend.

Use a placeholder conference name/branding, e.g., **"International Conference on Medical, Life & Health Sciences (ICMLHS 2026)"** — clearly mark it as replaceable content wherever it appears.

Build as a responsive, accessible, production-quality site with the **same light/dark theme toggle** as the corporate site.

### 4.2 SUBMIT ABSTRACT PAGE

Use this content, structured into clearly scannable sections (headings, numbered lists, and an accordion where helpful):

**Page hero:** "Abstract Submission Guidelines"
**Intro:** Stream Conferences invites researchers, clinicians, academicians, and industry leaders to submit original abstracts for oral, poster, and virtual presentations at our upcoming global events. All submissions undergo a rigorous double-blind peer review by our Scientific Advisory Board to ensure high academic and professional standards.

**1. Important Dates** (render as a timeline or date-card row — feed the same dates into the homepage Calendar widget):
- Early-Bird Abstract Submission Deadline: [Date]
- Final Abstract Submission Deadline: [Date]
- Notification of Acceptance: Within 5–7 business days after submission
- Presenter Registration Deadline: [Date]

**2. Submission Categories & Tracks** — reuse the Tracks pill component:
Medical & Clinical Sciences · Pharmaceutical & Life Sciences · Engineering & Technology · Academic & Research · Science & Medical Technologies

**3. Presentation Formats** (4 cards):
- Keynote / Plenary Talk — reserved for distinguished speakers and senior authorities (25–30 minutes).
- Oral Presentation — standard research presentation (15–20 minutes, including Q&A).
- Poster Presentation — visual display during dedicated poster sessions with live interactive Q&A.
- Virtual / Online Presentation — recorded or live-streamed presentation for remote delegates.

**4. Abstract Formatting Requirements** (structured checklist/spec block):
- Title: Maximum 25 words. Concise, descriptive, Title Case.
- Author Details: Full names, institutional affiliations, city, country, and email of presenting author and co-authors; mark corresponding/presenting author with an asterisk (*).
- Word Limit: 250–350 words (excluding title, authors, affiliations).
- Keywords: 3–6 relevant keywords, semicolon-separated.
- Core Structure: Background/Objectives · Methods/Methodology · Results/Findings · Conclusion/Significance (briefly describe each, as in the source content).

**5. Submission Terms & Conditions** (accordion or bullet list):
- Originality — must be unpublished original research.
- Ethics Approval — human/animal studies must confirm compliance and institutional approval.
- Registration Requirement — at least one author must register to present and be published.
- Commercial Bias — promotional/commercial abstracts without scientific merit will be rejected.

**6. How to Submit** (numbered step component, 4 steps):
1. Prepare your abstract using the standard Stream Conferences Abstract Template (.docx format) — include a "Download Template" button.
2. Access the online submission portal via the "Submit Abstract" button on this page.
3. Fill out the required author information and select your primary presentation track and format.
4. Upload your abstract document and receive an automated confirmation email with your unique Submission ID.

**Primary CTA:** A prominent "Start Your Submission" button opening a multi-step submission form (Author Info → Track/Format Selection → File Upload → Review & Submit) or, if the tool doesn't support file upload flows, a clear form to `abstracts@streamconferences.com` with mocked submission handling.

Footer note: For inquiries or technical support regarding abstract submissions, contact abstracts@streamconferences.com.

### 4.11 FAQ PAGE

Render as a categorized Accordion using this exact content:

**Eligibility & General**
- Who is eligible to submit an abstract? — Abstract submissions are open to researchers, clinical physicians, academicians, industry professionals, postdoctoral fellows, and students from around the world. We welcome submissions across all career stages and multidisciplinary sectors aligned with our conference tracks.
- Can I submit more than one abstract? — Yes. You may submit multiple abstracts. However, each accepted abstract must have a unique registered author to present the work at the conference to avoid scheduling conflicts.
- Is there an abstract submission fee? — No. Submitting an abstract for review by our Scientific Advisory Board is completely free. Registration fees apply only after your abstract has been accepted for presentation.

**Format & Submission Process**
- What is the word limit and structure for the abstract? — Abstracts must be between 250 and 350 words (excluding title, author list, and affiliations). They should follow a structured format including Background/Objectives, Methods, Results, and Conclusion/Significance.
- Can I submit an abstract that has already been published? — No. We require original research that has not been published or accepted for publication in a peer-reviewed journal prior to the date of the conference.
- Can I edit my abstract after submitting it? — You can request modifications to your submitted abstract prior to the final submission deadline by contacting our team with your Submission ID. Revisions requested after the deadline will be subject to editorial review.

**Review & Acceptance**
- How are abstracts evaluated? — All submissions undergo a double-blind peer review by experts on our Scientific Advisory Board. Evaluation criteria include scientific rigor, originality, relevance to the track, clarity of methodology, and potential impact.
- When and how will I be notified of the decision? — Notifications of acceptance or rejection are sent via email within 5 to 7 business days following your submission. Please ensure your contact details are accurate and check your spam/junk folder if you have not received an update.
- Will accepted abstracts be published? — Yes. All accepted and presented abstracts will be published in the official Conference Proceedings digital book, complete with an assigned DOI/ISBN where applicable.

**Presentation & Registration**
- Do I need to register for the conference right away to submit? — No. You do not need to register before submitting your abstract. You only need to register after receiving your formal acceptance notification.
- What happens if I cannot attend the conference in person? — We offer a dedicated Virtual Presentation option. Remote presenters can participate live online or submit a pre-recorded session, allowing their work to be included in the proceedings and presented to the global audience.
- What if I need a visa to attend in person? — Once your abstract is accepted and your conference registration is completed, our organizing committee will issue an Official Acceptance & Visa Invitation Letter to support your visa application.

End with: "Need Further Assistance? If you have additional questions regarding abstract guidelines or encounter technical issues during submission, please reach out to our scientific coordination team at abstracts@streamconferences.com." — with a "Contact Us" button.
